TasteTrace - MVP One-Pager
Helping people connect food to symptoms, effortlessly.
The Problem
Millions suffer from unexplained symptoms - bloating, headaches, fatigue, skin flare-ups - potentially
caused by food sensitivities. But identifying which foods are the culprits is time-consuming,
frustrating, and often inconclusive.
- Manual food journals are tedious
- Symptom onset is often delayed
- Medical tests are expensive and imprecise
Our Solution
TasteTrace is a low-friction mobile app that helps users discover links between what they eat and
how they feel - without complicated inputs or behavior change.
Core MVP Features
1. Quick-Tap Logging:
Users log meals in seconds using pre-saved "meal tiles" (e.g., "Oatmeal," "Turkey Wrap,"
"Smoothie") with auto-timestamping and optional notes/photos.
2. Daily Smart Symptom Check-Ins:
A single push notification asks users, "Did you feel any symptoms today?"
- Tap "Yes" symptom picker (type + severity)
- App links symptom to meals in the prior 6 12 hours
Value Proposition
"Feel better, faster - without complicated tests. TasteTrace helps you identify which foods may be
triggering your symptoms, using just a few seconds a day."
Target Audience
- Health-conscious adults with chronic or unexplained symptoms
- People with IBS, migraines, autoimmune flares, or fatigue
- Parents of symptomatic children
- Users trying elimination diets
- Nutritionists and functional medicine providers (future B2B)
Why This MVP Works
Design Principle | Implementation | Impact
------------------------|------------------------------------|------------------------------------
Frictionless UX | 1-tap meal logs + smart reminders | High retention, low abandonment
Pattern Engine | Correlates time-stamped data | Personalized insights over time
Rapid Build Cycle | Lightweight frontend/backend | Ready for launch & user feedback
MVP Metrics to Track
- Daily Active Users (DAU)
- Log Completion Rate (Meals + Symptoms)
- Insight Trigger Rate (e.g., "X food linked to Y symptom")
- User Retention (Day 1, 7, 30)
